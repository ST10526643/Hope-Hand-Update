About HopeHands Foundation

HopeHands Foundation is a fictional humanitarian organisation created for this coursework project. The site demonstrates community-support content about food, clothing, education and skills development.

Our mission

To help vulnerable communities with food, clothing, education and skills development.

Our vision

We want every person to have basic needs, opportunities and dignity.

Our values

Respect, honesty, teamwork and helping others.
